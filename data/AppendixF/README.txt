Columns Definitions for AppendxForiginal.csv
State: refers to the state which a crew operates in, takes a single value. This will be left blank for certain crews. How useful this column is depends, essentially it exist for the scenarios where multiple crews per state have the same designated REGIONAL herbarium, such as Arizona. If/when arizona adds more crews, they should inherit the REGIONAL herbarium.
CollectorCode: the collection code used by an SOS team. Currently (at time of repo creation in 2024) it seems that only active codes are used. A few wildcards are used in here for states which are anticipated to have more crews and which will inherit the REGIONAL herbarium. Perhaps this usage should be dropped and referred to soley in the STATE field.
HerbariumUse: Refers to the SOS voucher location designation. National is assumed as US for all crews here, although this may change with incorporation of Hawaii who are better served by NTBG - thats beyond my purview. REGIONAL refers to a prominent herbarium which has a range of collections for a geographic region. LOCAL refers to a, generally smaller, herbarium more proximal to the crews area of operation.
Priority: Certain areas have fluctuations in the usage of herbaria, as their curators change. This indicates in which order crews should try and deposit specimens, for most areas 1 is assumed explicitly, while in Colorado extreme variation is encountered.

Notes on digitizing. These data were digitized by Reed Benkendorf in late 2024 and early 2025, from APPENDIX F in the 2024 SOS manual. RB added a few crew specifications which were developed in 2024, and he was familiar with - see UT070 UT080 CO130 and C0150, BRY was assumed the regional herbarium for UT030.
